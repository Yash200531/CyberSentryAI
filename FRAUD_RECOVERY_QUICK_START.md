# Fraud Recovery Assistant - Quick Start Guide

## What is the Fraud Recovery Assistant?

The Fraud Recovery Assistant is an AI-powered feature that helps you recover from cyber threats like phishing, scams, malware, identity theft, and financial fraud. It provides personalized step-by-step guidance to help you take control of the situation.

---

## How to Use It

### From a Threat Detection Report

1. **Run a Scan**: Scan any suspicious text, URL, email, or image
2. **View Results**: If a threat is detected, view the detailed report
3. **Click "GET RECOVERY HELP"**: Orange button in the top-right of the report
4. **Follow the Recovery Plan**: Complete each step as recommended

### Direct Access

1. **Navigate to Recovery Assistant**: Click the "Recovery Assistant" link in the left sidebar
2. **First Time**: You'll be prompted to run a scan first
3. **With Detected Threat**: You'll see your personalized recovery plan

## Understanding Your Recovery Plan

### Incident Summary
Shows the threat type, confidence level, and urgency priority:
- 🔴 **Critical**: Act immediately
- 🟠 **High**: Act within hours
- 🟡 **Medium**: Act within a day
- 🔵 **Low**: Take precautions

### Progress Tracker
Visual bar showing how many steps you've completed. Your goal is to reach 100%!

### Personalized Advice
AI-generated guidance specific to your situation, explaining:
- What information might be at risk
- Immediate actions to take
- How to protect yourself going forward

### Immediate Actions
The 3 most critical steps to take right now. These are marked as HIGH PRIORITY.

### Complete Recovery Checklist
All recommended steps, with options to:
- ✅ **Mark Complete**: Done this step
- ⏭️ **Skip**: Not applicable or will do later

### Important Resources
Contact information for:
- Emergency services (FBI, FTC, Anti-Phishing Group)
- Credit bureaus (for identity protection)
- Financial institutions

## Recovery Steps by Threat Type

### 🎣 Phishing
**What to do:**
1. Change all passwords immediately
2. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
3. Contact your bank if you shared financial info
4. Monitor accounts for unauthorized activity
5. Report to authorities

**Time to complete:** 1-2 hours

### 💰 Scam
**What to do:**
1. Stop all communication with scammer
2. Document everything
3. Report to FBI IC3 and FTC
4. Dispute fraudulent charges
5. Place fraud alerts

**Time to complete:** 2-3 hours

### 🦠 Malware
**What to do:**
1. Disconnect from internet
2. Run full antivirus scan
3. Change passwords from clean device
4. Remove suspicious programs
5. Update all software

**Time to complete:** 2-4 hours

### 🆔 Identity Theft
**What to do:**
1. Place fraud alert with credit bureaus
2. File police report
3. Report to FTC
4. Close compromised accounts
5. Review all credit reports

**Time to complete:** 3-5 hours

### 💳 Financial Fraud
**What to do:**
1. Contact bank/card company immediately
2. Dispute all fraudulent transactions
3. File police report
4. Place fraud alerts
5. Enable transaction monitoring

**Time to complete:** 2-3 hours

## Prevention Tips

### For Phishing
- Always verify sender email addresses
- Hover over links before clicking
- Never share passwords via email
- Look for grammar/spelling errors
- Be suspicious of urgent requests

### For Scams
- Verify identities through official channels
- Never send money to unknown people
- Don't trust caller ID alone
- Research before purchasing
- Trust your instincts

### For Malware
- Keep antivirus updated
- Don't download from untrusted sources
- Keep software up to date
- Use strong passwords and 2FA
- Back up data regularly

### For Identity Theft
- Monitor credit reports regularly
- Use unique passwords for each account
- Enable 2FA everywhere possible
- Shred sensitive documents
- Be cautious sharing personal info

### For Financial Fraud
- Monitor accounts regularly
- Enable transaction alerts
- Use secure payment methods
- Verify website security (HTTPS)
- Never share PINs or CVV codes

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How long does recovery take?
**A:** Depends on the threat type, but typically 1-5 hours for immediate actions, with ongoing monitoring for 30-90 days.

### Q: Do I need to complete all steps?
**A:** Recommended, but you can skip steps that don't apply to your situation.

### Q: Is my data private?
**A:** Yes, recovery plans are generated on-demand and not permanently stored. Data stays in your browser.

### Q: Can I access my plan later?
**A:** Currently, plans are session-based. We recommend downloading the report for your records.

### Q: What if I need more help?
**A:** Contact the emergency resources provided, or consult with a cybersecurity professional.

### Q: Is this legal advice?
**A:** No, this is general guidance. For legal matters, consult an attorney.
